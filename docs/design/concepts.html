The idea

Freezer tape, made smart

Everyone labels freezer bags the same way: masking tape, a marker pen, a date. We keep that one familiar gesture as the brand's signature and set everything around it in cool, quiet, highly legible type. Yellow tape always means "look at this": food to use first, a field to check, a sticker that's ready.

Readable at the freezer door

Big type, two huge buttons, high contrast in a dim garage. Atkinson Hyperlegible for everything you read, every text colour checked to WCAG AA.

Never guesses

The AI's best reading is shown for you to confirm. Uncertain fields wear the yellow tape; missing dates stay blank. No food-safety verdicts, only facts like "Frozen 5 months ago".

Shared by default

Everyone in the household sees the same freezer, live. Every change has a name and a time, and every removal can be undone.

Colour and type

Frost, ink and one strip of tape

Does "Freezer tape" feel right: calm, cool, with one bit of character? Or should it be warmer and more homely?

The name

Keep "What's in There?" for now? It drives the wordmark, the icon and, later, the domain on every sticker.
